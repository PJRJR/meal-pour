Meal Pour

How much of a day's nutrition does one meal use up? Based on the FDA's 2,000 calorie reference.

How to use it

  • Click a food to pour a serving. Click it again to add more.
  • Each ball is 1% of a daily value. A full bin is 100%. Red means over a limit, and green means a goal was met.
  • Hover over a ball to see which food it came from.

Food data: Nutrition details for most common foods (Kaggle), loaded from this Google Sheet.

Daily values: FDA Daily Value on the Nutrition and Supplement Facts Labels.
